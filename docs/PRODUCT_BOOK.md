# Product book

Patrata screens **4 products and 9 variants**. Each variant has its own rules, compiled from
lenders' published eligibility pages and RBI rules. ‡ marks Patrata's own policy assumption where no single
published figure exists. Generated from `backend/app/products.json`.

> Indicative criteria compiled from lenders' published eligibility pages and RBI rules. Each lender's real policy differs; values marked as assumptions are Patrata's own policy choices where no single published figure exists.

## Personal loan (unsecured)

Unsecured loan for any purpose. Decided mainly on income, job stability, credit score and existing EMIs.

### Salaried personal loan

**For:** Salaried and government employees who need a fixed EMI loan.

**Features:** Fixed EMI from the first month; No collateral; Tenure up to 5 years.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Who can apply | Salaried, Government |
| Age | 21+ today; at most 60 when the loan ends |
| Minimum income | ₹25,000 a month |
| Job or business years | 1+ |
| CIBIL | 700+ clear, 650 minimum |
| EMI burden (FOIR) | up to 50% clear, 60% maximum ‡ |
| Amount | ₹50,000 to ₹40 lakh ‡ |
| Tenure | 12 to 60 months |
| Indicative rate | 10.5% to 24% a year ‡ |

**Documents:** PAN and Aadhaar, Last 3 salary slips, 6 months' salary-account statement.

**Sources:**
- [BankBazaar: most lenders want age 21 to 60 and CIBIL 650+](https://www.bankbazaar.com/personal-loan-eligibility.html)
- [IndusInd: salaried minimum Rs 25,000 a month](https://www.bankbazaar.com/indusind-personal-loan-eligibility-calculator.html)
- [HDFC: up to Rs 40 lakh, up to 60 months, 1 year with current employer](https://www.bankbazaar.com/hdfc-personal-loan-eligibility-calculator.html)
- [ICICI: CIBIL 700+](https://www.icici.bank.in/personal-banking/loans/personal-loan/eligibility/eligibility-faqs)

## Home loan (secured)

Long loan secured on the property. Limited by RBI's loan-to-value caps, the applicant's age at the end of the loan, and EMI burden.

### Salaried home loan

**For:** Salaried and government employees buying or building a home.

**Features:** Up to 30 years; Lowest interest of all retail loans; Loan capped at a share of the property value (RBI).

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Who can apply | Salaried, Government |
| Age | 21+ today; at most 60 when the loan ends |
| Minimum income | ₹25,000 a month ‡ |
| Job or business years | 2+ ‡ |
| CIBIL | 700+ clear, 650 minimum ‡ |
| EMI burden (FOIR) | up to 50% clear, 65% maximum |
| Amount | ₹5 lakh to ₹5 crore ‡ |
| Tenure | 60 to 360 months |
| Loan-to-value | RBI: 90% up to ₹30 lakh, 80% for ₹30 to 75 lakh, 75% above |
| Indicative rate | 8% to 10.5% a year |

**Documents:** PAN and Aadhaar, Last 3 salary slips and Form 16, 6 months' bank statement, Property papers and sale agreement.

**Sources:**
- [RBI LTV caps: 90% up to Rs 30 lakh, 80% for Rs 30 to 75 lakh, 75% above](https://homefirstindia.com/blog/article/rbi-guidelines-for-home-loan)
- [Maximum tenure 30 years](https://homefirstindia.com/blog/article/rbi-guidelines-for-home-loan)

## How every variant is checked

1. Employment type allowed  2. Age today  3. Age when the loan ends  4. Minimum income  5. Job or business years
6. CIBIL (first-time borrowers are referred, never declined only for no history)  7. EMI burden on the EMI the
variant really charges  8. Amount limits  9. Tenure limits  10. Loan-to-value (home: RBI caps; vehicle and consumer:
share of price)  11. Repayment risk from the model trained on 307,511 real loans.

Any **fail** declines the application; any **review** sends it to a credit officer; all **pass** approves it
(when the approval model is used, it must also agree).
